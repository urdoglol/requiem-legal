Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data requiem collects, why it collects it, and how you can request its removal.

1. What We Collect

The Bot collects and stores the minimum data needed to operate its features:

  • Discord User IDs — to track economy balances, XP, warnings, reminders, and other per-user data
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs — to store per-server configuration settings
  • Discord Channel and Role IDs — for features such as logging, welcome messages, and auto-roles
  • Message content — only where explicitly required (e.g. sticky messages, auto-responders, snipe commands). This data is not retained beyond its functional purpose.
  • Moderation records — warnings, bans, mutes, and case history tied to user IDs

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Usernames, email addresses, or passwords
  • Payment or financial information
  • Personal information beyond what Discord provides via its API
  • Message content in bulk or outside of features that explicitly require it

3. How Data Is Used

Data is used solely to provide the Bot's features (economy, moderation, levels, games, etc.). We do not share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as it is needed to provide the Bot's features. Economy and level data persists until a user requests deletion. Snipe and ghostping data is temporary and cleared regularly. Server configuration data is removed when the Bot leaves a server.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us through our support server. We will remove your user data (economy, XP, moderation history, etc.) within a reasonable time following a verified request.
